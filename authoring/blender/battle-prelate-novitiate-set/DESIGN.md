# Novitiate Field Harness

A level-one field kit for the existing male Battle Prelate. The older face, body proportions, canonical rig and hammer stay the same. The secondary armor has compact single-lame shoulders, a low open collar, a plain breastplate, fitted leather-backed arm protection, plain knees and practical boots. A short russet cloth tabard, leather belt and one small faith token keep the class recognizable. Rolled edges are iron rather than gilded; brass is confined to one small token and functional buckles.

Nine modules: head (low gorget), shoulders, chest, hands, waist, legs, feet, back and tabard. No censer, oversized sacred tome, skull medallions, gold fringe, high shoulder guards or dense purity seals. Finish: subdued worked steel, dark iron edges, worn umber leather, muted red-brown cloth.

All meshes derive from the user-accepted explicit source cages or newly specified vertex patches. Original source files and game assets remain in the reference-rebuild package. No primitive substitute or procedural body generator is used. New source records retain explicit coordinates, UV corners, finishes, and derivation notes.

Review requires a clearly simpler silhouette and ornament hierarchy, closed evaluated shells, no naked attachment gaps, unchanged rig rest matrices, and compatible nine-clip motion. The unchanged character body and current warhammer are shared across sets.
