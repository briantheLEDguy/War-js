Source files are JSON: `{schema_version:1, component:string, reference_notes:[], parts:[]}`. Each part has `id`, `vertices:[{id:string,co:[x,y,z]}]`, `faces:[{id:string,vertices:[vertexId,...],uv:[[u,v],...],material:string}]`, `modifiers:[]`, `landmarks:{name:vertexId}`, `seams:[[id,id],...]`, `sharp_edges:[[id,id],...]`, `creases:[{edge:[id,id],value:0..1}]`, `closed:bool`, `transform:{location:[x,y,z],rotation_degrees:[x,y,z],scale:[x,y,z]}`. Optional `instances` are an explicit finite list of transforms applied to the same authored component.

Permitted modifiers: `{type:"MIRROR",axis:"X"}`, `{type:"SUBSURF",levels:2}`, `{type:"BEVEL",width:0.001,segments:2}`, `{type:"SOLIDIFY",thickness:0.003,offset:0}`. Faces must have consistent winding. Blender may recalculate normals as a finishing operation. Open control surfaces are allowed when declared; evaluated Solidify shells are checked closed. Materials are named skin, lip, eye_white, iris, pupil, brow, steel, brass, dark_steel, leather, crimson, parchment. Explicit vertices/faces only: no primitives, body templates, shape sweeps, sampled curves, scatter, interpolation, trigonometric coordinate recipes, or automatic topology generation. A preparation script may serialize literal authored tables and resolve names/UV records; it must not invent shape coordinates or face connectivity.

The loader only reads these records, applies listed modifiers, assigns materials, and serializes evaluated geometry. Proof is static and unrigged. No milestone is accepted by successful import or a triangle count.
