# Cinderfen quartermaster

Original Greenskin supply vendor Vask Rauth for Cinderfen Outskirts. Identity,
title, vendor role, coordinates and services are preserved. The production board
specifies Greenskin; the old roster Chaos model was placeholder presentation.

Isolated sources, generating tools, textures, three GLB exports and byte-bound
review evidence live here. Exact fitting and anatomical input provenance is
retained without duplicating historical release trees. No primitive models.

Build with Blender 5.0 --background --threads 2 --python-exit-code 1 and
--python tools/build_inhabitants.py. Refresh tools/inspect_master.py through
Blender, then run python tools/run_quality.py. All current-byte technical gates
and actual-import front/head/side/rear/run_side/death:2 views must pass before
freeze. WORK_STATE.md is the readiness authority; unfinished files are candidates.
Root owns game, GM, registry and campaign publication.

The waistcoat is a continuous sleeveless pattern fitted over linen and the
trouser waist, with real thickness, two formed pockets and brass hook clasps.
Its cut loops are relaxed on the supporting garment; the collar opening leaves
load-bearing shoulder bridges. A source rest preflight catches construction
intersections early. It does not replace the actual-export layer-clearance gate,
which includes the complete linen/trouser surfaces and exposed anatomical skin
at rest and every exported clip key/midpoint. Ledger suspension is tested at both
ends of both closed straps in the source and all three animated exports.

`quartermaster_waistcoat.py` derives the outer garment from the finished shirt
surface and its evaluated skin fields, with a separate physical thickness.
`quartermaster_arm_motion.py` fits forward action gestures and bent gait arms
around the broad retained torso. `quartermaster_locomotion.py` places the fall
brace in the moving chest frame; palm contact fitting only lowers a hand once
its brace reaches the floor. These derivatives retain the nine named clips.
The `probe_*` tools produce diagnostic witnesses only and cannot establish
readiness or replace the full imported-mesh checks.

Existing arm-volume, motion, garment, footwear and continuity thresholds remain
unchanged. Thirteen export regression tests are required. Reports are compact
and tied to current model/source hashes. Standing approval covers aesthetics;
completed technical evidence remains necessary before publication.
