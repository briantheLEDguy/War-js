# Universal field apothecary

The station makes preparation and drying readable at village scale. An open
central worktop faces the player; two shallow trays and a worked stoneware mortar
occupy the outer halves. A narrow back shelf holds individually shaped amber and
green stoppered vials in fitted timber cups with leather restraints. Three herb
bundles hang below the rear crossbar, with distinct curled leaves and stems.

Tapered pegged legs, shouldered stretchers, cut receiving mortises, shaped knees,
breadboard ends and recessed fastening pins carry the craftsmanship. Restrained
surface wear follows timber grain and the actual preparation area. There is no
realm insignia: regional architecture remains responsible for the exterior theme.

The front working route stays empty. Colliders describe actual structural masses
and worktop; small herbs, stoppers and rivets are not broad movement blockers.
Flat feet define ground Y0. No floor or invisible room-sized collision is added.

LOD0 keeps joinery, leaf curl, bindings and vial rims; LOD1 reduces curved section
samples; LOD2 retains table, rack, trays and readable bundle/vial silhouettes while
removing only small incisions, thread detail and secondary leaflets.
