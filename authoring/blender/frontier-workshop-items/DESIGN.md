# Frontier siege workshop items

Two separate human-scale props for keep courtyards and workshop bays. Their
material and construction reference is the published Sunmeadow/Cinderfen
workshop and frontier catapult/ram: fitted oak, shaped dark iron, practical hemp
lashings and muted mineral loads. Those renders were inspected before design.
No city/siege geometry or painted pixels are reused.

The repair bench has a 0.94 m work surface, four individually crowned planks,
cross-grain end boards, shouldered stretchers seated in real leg mortises and
driven pins. A left-offset forged vise leaves the middle frontage usable. Its
moving jaw, lead screw and sliding handle have explicit receiving sockets.
A fitted rear tool-rest supports a hammer/chisel; loose tongs and a worn repair
strap make the work area legible without covering the entire tabletop.

The ammunition cradle has chamfered sled feet, a joined timber deck, a low front
retainer and stronger back/side bracing. Separately cut angular stone loads sit
in the crib, with original cleavage surveys and restrained edge wear. Rope
bindings follow fitted corner routes and retain visible twisted strands nearby.
Keep the front accessible for loading; no floor-sized invisible movement wall.

Source coordinates are metres, Z-up, front -Y. Runtime is Y-up, front +Z.
Collision will be derived conservatively from actual support masses and checked
at each LOD; work/approach clearance is a separate contract. These are static
workshop props and do not claim animated repair or ammunition gameplay.

Every visible piece starts as an explicit section/path, plate boundary or stone
facet survey. There are no primitive operators or stock placeholder blocks.
Retain original cages, material graphs, source UVs and three deliberate LODs.
Source-direction review precedes final PBR bake/export/reimport acceptance.
