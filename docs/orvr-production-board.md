3. Complete Brightfen and Ashen, Glassriver and Gorepine, Highvale and Obsidian,
   with the remaining lairs.
4. Complete Dawnline, Shatterline, both Crownworks and both fortresses.

release are integrated. Remaining work is recorded in the plan and verification
record; no zone is declared art-complete yet. Hosted deployment is a separate
operational action and has not been performed.
