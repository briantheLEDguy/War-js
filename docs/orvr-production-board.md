This board tracks execution of [the approved plan](world-orvr-implementation-plan.md).
An asset counts as delivered only after source, export, material, animation where
applicable, visual and runtime checks. A finished asset package does not finish a zone.

   verify an advance and counterpush with the completed scenes.
2. Complete Greybrook and Bleakroot, then Ironwood and Vilemere, with their lairs.
3. Complete Brightfen and Ashen, Glassriver and Gorepine, Highvale and Obsidian,
   with the remaining lairs.
4. Complete Dawnline, Shatterline, both Crownworks and both fortresses.
5. Integrate both capital siege routes with shared navigation and finish campaign
   outcomes, recovery, multiplayer acceptance and representative performance checks.

The eighteen road networks and the first Sunmeadow terrain/architecture/nature
release are integrated. Remaining work is recorded in the plan and verification
record; no zone is declared art-complete yet. Hosted deployment is a separate
operational action and has not been performed.
