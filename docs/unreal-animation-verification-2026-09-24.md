# Supplied animation replacement: Windows development verification

The four equipped profiles are Battle Prelate, male Empire Sunfire Templar,
Warbrute and Ember Arcanist. The Templar uses the established human body and rig
with articulated plate, a blue tabard, sword, shield and original sun heraldry.
The Prelate's body and armor remain intact. Character-specific grip, contact,
stow and clearance corrections live beside the reusable animation recipes.

## Executed evidence

- **44 supplied FBXs, 40 abilities, 41 action variants:**
  `migration/supplied-animation-coverage.json` links every source hash to live
  player and participant-bot scenarios, all ability presentations, and captured
  gameplay images. Every source has recorded execution; previews alone do not
  qualify.
- **54 native tests passed**, including 82 ability executions, directional
  locomotion, turns, jump/landing, reactions, death, four production respawns,
  interruption, swept capsule travel and equipment handoffs. Report:
  `artifacts/unreal/editor/test-1790257762132-29348/index.json`.
- **548 gameplay images** were regenerated by that passing run. Their manifest
  is `unreal/AegisWar/Saved/AnimationGameplayCapture/frames.json`; image hashes
  are included in the public coverage manifest.
- **Multiplayer:** a dedicated server and two loopback clients each completed
  all 41 variants. The delayed client joined during an action. Identities,
  start times, durations and completed pose phases matched the authority.
  Report: `artifacts/unreal/animation-replacement/network/1790257556864-13848/report.json`.
- **11 playable/NPC rig sets:** 213 sequences and 14,904 sampled poses passed
  skeleton, finite-transform, limb-length, compression and recovery checks.
  Maximum measured compression error was 0.009555 cm. All 93 equipped motion
  roles passed the 30 Hz equipment ground-clearance measurements; stow handoff
  transforms also passed.
- **Removal:** 3,494 GLBs were scanned; 185 Blender files, including 47 backups,
  have verified character-track removal. All 286 retired native packages are
  absent, with zero superseded assets in the registry. NPC bindings in eight
  populated maps were checked. The standalone `Spell Cast.fbx` is deleted.
  Textual provenance remains in `migration/animation-removal.json`.

The contact review replaced generic strike fractions with measured hammer,
sword, cleaver and kick events. Stow transitions now carry equipment in the
hands before attachment to the back. Matched lighting/camera renders compare
the Templar and Prelate and sample equipped attacks, casting, stowing and
retrieval. This is a first-pass development model review; it does not grant
production art approval.

The late-join check exposed a tick-order defect: character movement could
request a pose before the pawn selected its current action time. Selection now
runs at animation preparation, and completed pose timestamps are published
after evaluation. Both native phase assertions and the multiplayer proof pass.

Icon of Wrath's native tests cover validated placement, ten-second duration,
allied living player/bot eligibility, actual hostile health damage, range and
occlusion, non-stacking overlap and ownership cleanup. Its 14-second cooldown,
15-mana cost and five-metre radius remain unchanged.

## Reproduction and scope

Use `docs/unreal-animation-import.md` for pipeline and verification commands.
`publish-animation-coverage.py` requires passing gameplay, current captures,
structural/equipment checks, multiplayer evidence and removal receipts before
publishing coverage. Native packages, supplied FBXs and generated review media
remain private/local; the public repository contains code, recipes and hash-bound
evidence references.

The Editor build, general tests, Unreal-tool tests, all three typechecks,
migration audit, world validation and model validation have passed. Steam,
Linux/macOS packaging and the existing release acceptance gates remain
unapproved. Full siege encounter/roster playtests remain tracked separately in
`docs/unreal-city-siege.md`.
